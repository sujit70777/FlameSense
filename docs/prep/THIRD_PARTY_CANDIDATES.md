clearly shown". Licenses were checked against the publisher's own statement (in the PDF/XML) and
against Crossref's license record. Only CC-licensed items were downloaded, and only from the
publisher, arXiv or Europe PMC. Nothing came from a shadow library or behind a paywall.

**NASA involvement column:** a source with NASA co-authors or NASA flight hardware still counts as a
third-party *publication*, but it is not independent of NASA. The judges will value the "no NASA"
rows most.

## Extracted rows

`manual/third_party_CANDIDATES.csv`: **6 rows** from Sharma et al. 2024, Table 1 (p.5). One row per
test, at the final flow step whose outcome the table states. Footnote c ("Flame sustained and
consumed the sample") gives `sustained=true`; footnote d ("Flame quenched within 10 s") gives
`sustained=false`. The atmosphere (1.0 atm, ~22% O₂) and sample thickness (SIBAL 0.33 mm, PMMA
1 mm) are values printed in the text (pp.3, 1), quoted in `source_text_snippet`. All rows are
`quality_flag=digitized`, `verified=no`, `source_type=third_party`. PMMA `fuel_class` is left blank
because the paper doesn't state thin/thick (`[LEAD]` to decide).
