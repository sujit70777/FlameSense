# FlameSense — Model card

Generated by `python -m flamesense.cli evaluate`. Do not edit by hand.

## Intended use
Research and education: exploring how oxygen, pressure, airflow, gravity and material affect whether a flame keeps burning and how fast it spreads, within the range NASA tested.

## Not intended for
Operational fire-safety decisions, material qualification, or certification of any spacecraft, habitat, building or vehicle. FlameSense is not a certified safety system.

## Data
Only real experiment runs from `data/raw/` and `data/manual/` with a source citation. No synthetic rows. Rows flagged `suspect` are excluded from training. No imputation: rows missing a selected feature are dropped.

Features used: flow_direction__concurrent, flow_direction__opposed, fuel_class__thick_solid, fuel_class__thin_solid, fuel_thickness_mm, log10_flow, log10_gravity, o2_mole_fraction, o2_partial_kpa, pressure_kpa.

## Limitations
- Small dataset: 42 usable runs for the burning model and 45 for spread rate.
- Test-series dependence: runs from one campaign share hardware and procedures.
- Extrapolation: outside the tested ranges (and at Moon or Mars gravity, where there is little or no data) predictions are extrapolations and are flagged as such.
- Physics model simplifications: the thin-fuel reference ignores finite-rate chemistry and radiation and over-predicts spread near extinction.
- No operational use: see 'Not intended for'.

## Ethical note
Fire safety affects lives. FlameSense shows data support and extrapolation warnings next to every prediction so that nobody mistakes an estimate for a safety guarantee.
