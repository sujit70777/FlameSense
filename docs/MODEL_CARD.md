## Limitations
- Small dataset: 0 usable runs for the burning model and 0 for spread rate.
- Test-series dependence: runs from one campaign share hardware and procedures.
- Extrapolation: outside the tested ranges (and at Moon or Mars gravity, where there is little or no data) predictions are extrapolations and are flagged as such.
- Physics model simplifications: the thin-fuel reference ignores finite-rate chemistry and radiation and over-predicts spread near extinction.
- No operational use: see 'Not intended for'.

## Ethical note
Fire safety affects lives. FlameSense shows data support and extrapolation warnings next to every prediction so that nobody mistakes an estimate for a safety guarantee.
